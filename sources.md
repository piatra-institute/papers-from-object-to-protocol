# Sources

## Structured bibliography (2026-09-23)

The inline reference list was migrated to references.yaml (CSL) and in-text citations to Pandoc [@id] syntax. None of the legacy entries carried a DOI and Crossref was not usable for most of these books and web documents, so all 36 entries were entered by hand from their publication details; two carry DOIs (Bourdieu 1996, 10.1515/9781503615861; Strathern 1988, 10.1525/california/9780520064232.001.0001). The web sources were retrieved on 2026-09-23: the Whitney artport page for *xhairymutantx*, the Serpentine page for *The Call*, the Sotheby's auction page for *Exorbitant Stage: Botto, a Decentralized AI Artist* (New York, 17-24 October 2024), the El País article of 8 April 2025 by Pablo G. Bejerano, the Federal Register text of the Copyright Office guidance (88 FR 16190, document 2023-05321), and the Art Blocks, fxhash and PromptBase documentation sites. The MoMA Fluxus and SFMOMA LeWitt pages refused automated retrieval and are entered without URLs.
Changes from the legacy list: the El País article is entered under its author (bejerano2025); the combined "Sol LeWitt wall drawing documentation" entry is split into SFMOMA and Whitney entries; a separate entry for the 1550 Torrentino edition of Vasari's *Lives* is added so that "Vasari's *Lives* (1550, 1568)" is cited to both editions; *Illuminations* (1968) was published by Harcourt, Brace & World (legacy: Schocken, which issued the 1969 paperback). Uncited legacy entries (Ono, Young, Weiner, the institutional documentation pages, Art Blocks, fxhash, PromptBase) are now cited at the sentences that draw on them.
